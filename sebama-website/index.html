Who We Are

Dedicated to Compassionate Community Care in Dharmapuri

Sebama Charitable Foundation, located in Sogathur, Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu, was established in 1988 with an initial focus on providing care and rehabilitation for leprosy-affected individuals and vulnerable families across the district. Over nearly four decades of grassroots service, the foundation expanded its humanitarian outreach through Mercy Home, creating a holistic residential care ecosystem for those in need.

Integrated Care

Bringing children & seniors together for intergenerational warmth.

100% Compliant

Registered with 12A, 80G, CSR-1 & NITI Aayog Darpan ID.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, stigma-free society where every child gains access to quality education, every senior citizen lives with comfort and dignity, and every marginalized family in rural Tamil Nadu receives the social security needed to thrive.

Our Mission

To restore dignity, health, and hope to society’s most vulnerable populations by offering integrated residential care for orphaned children and destitute senior citizens, while delivering vital medical, nutritional, and educational support to families affected by leprosy.

Our Core Pillars

How We Support the Vulnerable in Dharmapuri

We run dedicated residential homes and outreach initiatives providing shelter, health care, education, and emotional dignity.

Residential Home

Mercy Home for Children

Providing orphaned and underprivileged children with safe shelter, nutritious meals, formal schooling support, digital literacy, and character development.

Senior Home

Destitute Elderly Care

Offering peaceful shelter, daily nursing, regular health checkups, and loving companionship to abandoned elderly individuals in their golden years.

Outreach Program

Leprosy Family Rehabilitation

Delivering specialized medical supplies, monthly nutrition kits, educational scholarships, and stigma-reduction support to leprosy-affected families in the district.
